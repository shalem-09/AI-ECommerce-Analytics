# Dashboard design

- Executive page: net sales, gross profit, margin, completed orders, AOV, units, and month-over-month growth.
- Sales page: monthly net sales/gross profit trend, category contribution, regional sales, and payment method.
- Product page: top 10 products by net sales and units, profitability, and average discount.
- Customer page: RFM segment count/value, recency versus frequency scatter, and repeat customer rate.
- Operations page: status mix, cancellation rate, and return rate.
- Forecast page: monthly actuals, holdout model predictions, three-month linear-regression projection, MAE, RMSE, and MAPE.
- Add date, order status, category, state, and customer segment slicers. Use completed-order filters on revenue measures.
- Clearly label product cost and gross profit as estimates based on the current product cost; this is not historical COGS.
